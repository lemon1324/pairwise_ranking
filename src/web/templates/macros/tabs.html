{# The sheet tabs: how one project's four sheets are moved between. The same four links appear twice on every screen - in the top bar at desk widths, and in a sticky bar at the bottom of the phone layout, where the top bar has room for the project's name and nothing else. The stylesheet does the switching; both copies are always in the page. They are written once here rather than four times across Items, Compare, Rankings and Settings, because the part that is easy to get wrong is the same in all four: the current tab has to be marked with aria-current, and it has to be marked in *both* copies. There is a fifth state, and it is the one this file exists to be careful about: the drawing register has no project open. There is nothing to switch between and no project for the menu to be about, so `tabs` and `project_menu` draw nothing at all and the register's top bar carries the app name instead. Passing project_id=none is the way to say that. The addresses come from src.web.urls, not from url_for: three of the four screens are built in later chunks and url_for cannot name a route that does not exist yet.
